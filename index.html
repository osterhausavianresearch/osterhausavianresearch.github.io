Research Program

Migration Ecology for Conservation Applications

Artificial Light at Night

Quantifying behavioral responses of nocturnally migrating birds to light and evaluating strategies that minimize negative impacts.

Radar Aeroecology

Using weather and research radars to study the ecology of nocturnal avian migration, assess anthropogenic impacts to migrants, and devise techniques to conserve this imperilled system.

Acoustic Monitoring

Leveraging recent technological advacements and cutting-edge methods to study migration timing, behavior, and species-level movement patterns through the recording of nocturnal flight calls.

Conservation Science

Working with stakeholders to co-create knowledge and connect field observations, remote sensing, and quantitative tools to guide practical and actionable conservation.
