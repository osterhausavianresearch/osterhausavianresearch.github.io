Teaching & Mentoring

Training students to connect natural history, data, and conservation

My teaching interests center on field ecology, ornithology, conservation biology, quantitative ecology, and experiential learning. I strive to connect classroom learning to in-the-field experiences to give students an improved understanding of the intricacies and interconnections of ecological science and conservation.

Courses and experience

  • Instructor of Record: Avian Field Ecology lecture/lab.
  • Teaching Assistant: Avian Field Ecology lecture/lab.
  • Teaching Assistant: Intro to Cellular and Molecular Biology Lab.
  • Teaching Assistant: Undergraduate Student Capstone.
  • Teaching Assistant: Ichthyology Lab.

Teaching approach

I want students to leave ecology courses with usable skills: careful observation, field confidence, quantitative reasoning, and an understanding of how science informs conservation decisions.

Future course areas include ornithology, migration ecology, field methods, conservation biology, aeroecology, and data-centered ecological monitoring.
