Methods

Field, radar, and acoustic approaches for migration science

My work applies cutting edge approaches and utilizes a variety of datasets in the study of avian migration.

Radar Aeroecology

Weather and research radar provide large-scale views of aerial migration, including timing, intensity, direction, and altitude.

Nocturnal Flight Calls

Acoustic monitoring helps connect migration activity to species-level signals and local behavioral responses.

Field Ecology

Field surveys, carcass persistence work, and natural history observations ground remote sensing in ecological reality.

Conservation Evaluation

Project designs emphasize measurable outcomes for mitigation, monitoring, and management decisions.

Data Integration

Combining radar, acoustics, weather, community science, and field data can reveal patterns no single method captures alone.

Mentored Training

Future students can build projects around field methods, remote sensing, coding, conservation partnerships, or combinations of these skills.
