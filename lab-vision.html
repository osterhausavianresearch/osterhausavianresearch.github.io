Lab Vision

A collaborative lab for migration science and conservation support

Read below to understand my vision for my future lab.

Lab Mission

My lab will work in collaboration with conservation practitioners to co-create knowledge that advances our understanding of avian migration, explores how avian migrants are impacted by and are contending with anthropogenic alteration of their environment, develops actionable conservation strategies for the mitigation of these impacts and the improved conservation of avian migrants, and trains the next generation of researchers and conservation professionals.

Core Research Areas

  • Analysis of the impacts of anthropogenic threats to avian migration.
  • Radar and acoustic monitoring across scales to improve our understanding of migration ecology.
  • Development and refinement of the methods available to study nocturnal migration.
  • Decision support and co-development of data driven conservation action for agencies, cities, NGOs, and communities.

Student Projects

Students could pursue field-heavy, quantitative, remote-sensing, outreach, or conservation-partnership projects. I want to work with students and collaborators to design studies that give students the skills and experiences they need, and provide high quality research outputs to our collaborators that are useful in conservation decision making and push forward our understanding of avian migration.

Lab Values

I aim to build a lab culture that values careful research study, fully transparent analysis, practical conservation impact, inclusive mentoring, and generous collaboration.
