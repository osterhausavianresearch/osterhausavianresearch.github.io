About Me

Dylan M. Osterhaus

I am an ecologist studying avian migration and conservation. My work combines field experiments, radar, bioacoustics, and large ecological datasets to understand how birds move through changing environments.

Background

From aquatic systems to migration ecology

My training spans ecology and biodiversity, fisheries biology, and avian ecology. Before focusing on migration, I studied fish communities and restored oxbow wetlands. Across these systems, a common thread is using ecological research to answer practical conservation questions.
