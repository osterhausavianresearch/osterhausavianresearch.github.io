Research

Avian migration in the Anthropocene

My research seeks to understand how migratory birds respond to human-altered environments and how new monitoring approaches can make conservation action faster, more targeted, and more effective.

Research Themes

From ecological mechanisms to conservation decisions

I combine observational and experimental approaches to explain how migration responds to human activity and environmental variation—and to identify useful points of intervention.

01

Responses to anthropogenic and environmental change

I investigate how changes to the sensory, atmospheric, and physical environment influence movement and survival. My work examines how human activities and environmental stressors alter migratory behavior and the conditions birds encounter along their journeys. By combining field studies, experiments, and ecological monitoring, I seek to identify the mechanisms behind these responses and when they become consequential for conservation.

Related work: artificial light at night, extreme weather, and migration mortality.

02

From individuals to migration patterns

Using large-scale ecological datasets and field studies, I examine how age, life history, and migration strategy shape the timing and speed of fall migration. Differences among individuals accumulate into patterns in the composition of migrant communities across space and time. Understanding those patterns helps identify when and where particular demographic groups occur.

Related work: age-specific migration timing, migration speed, and long-term demographic change.

03

Migration science into conservation action

I connect migration monitoring with decisions about when and where to act. I integrate ecological evidence across species and scales to identify vulnerabilities, evaluate conservation strategies, and help target interventions where they can have the greatest benefit. The broader goal is to improve mitigation and make effective use of limited conservation resources.

Related work: migration forecasts, Lights Out alerts, and species-specific conservation coverage.
